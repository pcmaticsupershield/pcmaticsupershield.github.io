Common Problems During Installation

The Download Does Not Start

Check your internet connection and try the download again. You can also restart your browser and verify that your computer has enough available storage.

The Installer Cannot Be Found

Check your browser's download history or your Downloads folder. Browsers can save files to different locations depending on your settings.

The Account Does Not Show the Purchase

Confirm that you signed in using the email address associated with the original purchase. If you have multiple email addresses, it is easy to accidentally access a different account.

Installation Stops Unexpectedly

Restart the computer and try again. Close unnecessary applications and make sure Windows has enough free storage and a stable internet connection.

Do You Need to Download It Again?

If the software is already installed and functioning correctly, downloading it again may not be necessary. A new download is more relevant when you are installing on another computer, reinstalling after removing the program or replacing an existing Windows installation.
