Common Download Problems

The Download Does Not Start

Check your internet connection and refresh the account page. If necessary, try another supported browser and attempt the download again.

The Downloaded File Cannot Be Found

Check your browser's download history and open the folder containing the downloaded file. Your browser may use a different download location.

The Installer Does Not Open

Make sure the download completed successfully. If the file appears incomplete or damaged, obtain the current installer again from the appropriate source.

The Installation Stops

Verify your internet connection, available storage and Windows environment. Restarting the computer before trying the installation again can also help with temporary system problems.

Account Problems During Installation

If the software asks you to sign in during or after installation, use the same credentials associated with your existing purchase.

If your product information is missing after signing in, verify the email address you used. An incorrect account can make it appear that the purchase is unavailable.
