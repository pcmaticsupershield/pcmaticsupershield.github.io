Common Download Problems

The Download Option Is Not Visible

First confirm that you are signed in to the correct account. If you have multiple accounts, you may have accessed an account that does not contain the expected product information.

The Download Does Not Start

Check your internet connection and browser settings. You can also try refreshing the account page or using another supported browser.

The Installer Cannot Be Found

Check the Downloads folder and your browser's download history. The installer may have been saved to a different location if your browser uses a custom download folder.

Installation Stops

Check that your computer has sufficient storage, a stable internet connection and an appropriate Windows environment. Restarting the computer and trying the installation again can sometimes resolve temporary setup issues.
